## 🔑 Key Concepts

### Jira Terminology
- **Project**: Container for related issues
- **Issue**: Individual work item (story, task, bug)
- **Epic**: Large body of work
- **Sprint**: Time-boxed iteration (Scrum)
- **Board**: Visual representation of work
- **Workflow**: Lifecycle of an issue
- **Backlog**: Prioritized list of work

### Agile Methodologies
- **Scrum**: Sprint-based development
- **Kanban**: Continuous flow
- **Hybrid**: Mix of both approaches

## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Start Simple**: Don't over-complicate your setup
2. **Be Consistent**: Use standard practices across teams
3. **Communicate**: Keep discussions in Jira
4. **Groom Regularly**: Keep your backlog healthy
5. **Automate**: Eliminate repetitive tasks
6. **Measure**: Track metrics that matter
7. **Improve**: Regular retrospectives and adjustments
