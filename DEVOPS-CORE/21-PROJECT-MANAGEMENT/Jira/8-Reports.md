# Jira Reports

## Overview

Jira provides various reports to track progress, identify bottlenecks, and make data-driven decisions.

## Scrum Reports

### Burndown Chart
Shows remaining work in sprint over time.

```
Ideal: Work decreases steadily
Actual: May vary based on progress
```

### Velocity Chart
Shows story points completed per sprint.

```
Use for: Sprint planning and capacity estimation
```

### Sprint Report
Comprehensive sprint summary with completed and incomplete work.

### Epic Burndown
Tracks progress toward completing an epic.

## Kanban Reports

### Cumulative Flow Diagram
Shows distribution of work across workflow states.

### Control Chart
Analyzes cycle time and identifies outliers.

### Velocity Chart
Tracks throughput over time.

## Creating Custom Reports

Use Jira dashboards with gadgets to create custom views.
