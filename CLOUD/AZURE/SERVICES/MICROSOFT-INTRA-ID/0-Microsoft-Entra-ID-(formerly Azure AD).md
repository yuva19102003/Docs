
###  **Microsoft Entra ID**:
![Microsoft Entra ID Diagram](Pasted%20image%2020250205195151.png)

- A broader, more integrated identity and access management solution that includes **Azure AD** as a core component.
- Part of **Microsoft Entra**, a suite of identity and access management products designed for securing access across both cloud and hybrid environments.
- Focuses on managing identities across multiple systems, resources, and environments beyond just Azure.

---

### **Azure AD**:
![Azure AD Diagram](Pasted%20image%2020250205195415.png)

- A cloud-based identity and access management service for managing users, groups, and devices within Microsoft's cloud ecosystem (Azure).
- Primarily used for controlling access to Azure resources, applications, and services.

---
In short, **Azure AD** is part of **Microsoft Entra ID**, with the latter offering a more expansive suite of IAM tools.
